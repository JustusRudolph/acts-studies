Events and particles

The checkboxes of an event or folder select the particles in it that pass the filters. Click a particle's name to inspect it. ▸ next to a particle lists the tracks and seeds (≥ 2 of 3 space points from it; ↓TOF: below-TOF seed) with it as majority particle; they are drawn only when ticked. Efficiencies count the particles in the efficiency selection (the matching details).
